An original game inspired by classic 16‑bit castle artillery duels.

How to play

  • Aim — drag anywhere on the battlefield and pull back like a bow. Release to fire, or let go outside the aim zone to cancel. The sliders give you fine control.
  • Wind — the banner at the top shows the wind for this round. It pushes every cannonball sideways, so lead your shots into it.
  • Powder — every shot burns powder in proportion to its charge. Run dry and you must buy more before you can fire.
  • Gold — you earn taxes at the start of each round; a bigger, healthier castle pays better. Spend it in the shop on powder, cannons and masons.
  • Winning — bring down the enemy throne room, or leave them with no cannons and no coin to buy one.
  • Watch out — a hit on a powder magazine takes half the castle with it.
